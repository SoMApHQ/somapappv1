How SoMAp decides
  • Positions: learners with the same score share a position, and the next score takes the next number (100, 100, 99, 99, 97 → 1, 1, 2, 2, 3). Scores are compared to 2 decimal places, as on the report forms.
  • Academic: the Annual result from the report forms (January – November). To be ranked overall, a learner must have a result in every subject the class sat and at least 80% of the class's exams (the most exams any classmate sat). Subject awards use the same 80% for that subject.
  • Whole school: Pre-Primary (Baby, Middle, Pre-Unit) and Primary (Class 1 – 7) are ranked separately. Whole-school subject awards cover subjects taught in two or more classes.
  • Attendance: morning and afternoon sessions present ÷ sessions recorded, from 1 January to today. Whole-school attendance counts only learners marked from their class's first register day. Class awards for newer learners count from the day they joined, and the certificate says so.
