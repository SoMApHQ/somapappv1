Best Teachers of the Year

How SoMAp decides
  • The classes, streams and subjects each teacher saved in Worker Tasks → Class & Subjects are used.
  • For each one, SoMAp takes the annual result (the same report-form formula, January – November) of every learner who sat that subject and averages them. A stream counts only its own learners.
  • Teacher's average = all their class-subject averages added up ÷ number of class-subjects.
  • Pre-Primary teaching (Baby, Middle, Pre-Unit) and Primary teaching (Class 1 – 7) are ranked separately. Ties share a position.

Best Workers of the Year

How SoMAp decides (1 January → today, from the sign-in records)
  • Presence 40%: days signed in ÷ school work days (days any worker signed in).
  • Punctuality 30%: sign-ins on time (before 07:30) ÷ days signed in. Lateness with approved permission is not counted as a letter.
  • Sign-out 15%: days signed out ÷ days signed in.
  • Protocol 15%: starts at 100% and loses 5 points per explanation letter (late, absence, missed or early sign-out, late lesson plan).
  • Workers with fewer than 10 sign-ins are not ranked, but they still get the appreciation certificate.
