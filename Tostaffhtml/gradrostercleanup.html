Graduation Roster Cleanup

Scans enrollments/{year} and years/{year}/enrollments (root and Socrates-scoped mirrors) for promotion overrides that were duplicated into future years by a bug in the Promotion/Demotion tool — the exact defect that made already-graduated students (e.g. Class 7 in 2025) keep reappearing as "Class 7" in 2026, 2027, 2028.

Detection rule: within a group of enrollment records for the same student written by the same promotion action (same internal updatedAt timestamp), any year after the earliest one that repeats that action's original class is flagged as a duplicate. Records without a matching duplicate, or written by separate/legitimate actions, are left untouched.
