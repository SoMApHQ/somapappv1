Shifted Students Hub

How a shifted learner's debt is worked out (2026 and later)
  • Shifted in January: the learner never studied that year, so no school fee and no transport for that year is charged. Earlier-year debts stay as they are.
  • Shifted later: the learner owes every instalment of their class plan or personal agreement whose window had opened by the shift date (monthly plans count the shift month; after 10 Oct the whole year is due), minus what they paid.
  • Transport: months used up to the shift month, minus approved transport payments, are added to the school-fee debt.
"Recorded" is the figure saved on the day of the shift. "Fair debt" is calculated with the rules above. Records from 2025 and earlier are never recalculated.
